Schedule

Choose how many days each week you can study consistently. The aim is not to rush through a lesson, but to give it enough attention to read, write, listen, speak and return to anything that did not settle the first time.

Build your plan

Select up to seven days. The plan assumes one 30–45 minute session on each chosen day.

The practical kit

What you actually need

No specialist equipment and no paid subscription are required.

One ruled exercise book

An ordinary A4 or A5 ruled notebook is enough. A margin is useful for corrections.

Your phone’s voice recorder

Record in a quiet room and save the first and second readings separately.

L01-leccion-primera-first-readingL01-leccion-primera-second-reading

Optional, not essential

Headphones make comparisons easier. A pencil, one pen and a single highlighter are plenty.

The useful missing item is a routine, not another purchase: book open, notebook ready, recorder available, 30–45 focused minutes.
