How Poco a Poco works

The book begins with concrete objects, animals, pictures and direct questions. Vocabulary and forms are introduced in Spanish and repeatedly reused. The experiment adds handwriting, recordings, conversation and reflection without replacing Hall Avilés’ original progression.

Target: complete each lesson actively rather than simply reading through it.

Materials

One public-domain primary text, a notebook and a voice recorder are enough.
