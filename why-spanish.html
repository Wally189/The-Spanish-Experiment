THE REASON FOR THE EXPERIMENT

Why Spanish?

Because Spanish is both immediately useful and large enough to become a lifelong education: a living language of neighbours, friends, travel, literature, prayer and history.

Why this method?

The experiment is not built around collecting apps or memorising isolated phrases. It begins with an old direct-method course that lets the learner meet Spanish through Spanish itself.

Poco a Poco is especially attractive because its first pages are already readable. Pictures, objects, questions and repeated structures allow meaning to emerge before every sentence is dissected.

The governing idea: read, copy, answer aloud, listen back, use the lesson with real Spanish speakers, and record honestly what happens.

Why make it public?

A public experiment creates a record rather than a claim. It shows the work, the mistakes, the corrections and the gradual accumulation of ability. Other learners can follow the same public-domain source without needing to pretend that progress is effortless.
